Challenges & Roadmap · Items 12 & 13
Difficulties Fixed & What's Next
CHALLENGES WE FACED & SOLVED
1
🖼 Large Image Memory Overflow
20MB+ photos broke WebGL GPU memory limits
✔ Fix: Canvas bilinear downscale for AI, original for download
2
📷 Side-Profile Faces Missed
Faces at 30°+ angles not detected initially
✔ Fix: 68-pt landmark normalization + tolerance slider
3
📱 Auth Card Mobile Clipping
Sliding panel animation clipped on small screens
✔ Fix: Framer Motion subgrids + overflow-hidden bounds
4
⏱ AI Model Load Delay ~800ms
TF.js weights caused first-load lag
✔ Fix: Lazy-load with animated skeleton — zero perceived wait
